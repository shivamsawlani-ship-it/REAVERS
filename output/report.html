Concrete Case Studies: Why Physics & V2X Validation Are Essential

CASE-01: In-Range Spoofed Deceleration Command (0x200)
Scenario: Adversary injects 0x200 demanding 7.0 m/s² deceleration while vehicle cruises at 78 km/h.
AI Standalone: MISSED. Feature vector (mean ID, payload entropy) remained within standard operating range. Frame forwarded.
CAVShield Cyber-Physical Fusion: BLOCKED. Physics engine detected UNJUSTIFIED_DRIVER_BRAKE (pedal was 0.0%, difference > tolerance). Severity: 0.90.
Safety Guarantee: Prevented catastrophic unintended rear-end collision on highway.
CASE-02: Legitimate AEB Trigger With Real Radar Obstacle (Hard Negative)
Scenario: Sudden obstacle in ego lane at 30m causes autonomous emergency braking at 6.5 m/s².
AI Standalone: FALSE ALARM. Sudden statistical shift triggered high anomaly score (>0.85). Mode escalated, legitimate emergency braking blocked.
CAVShield Cyber-Physical Fusion: FORWARDED (PASSED). Physics engine cross-referenced radar sensor snapshot (track confirmed in-lane at 30m, TTC=1.36s), confirming braking was justified.
Safety Guarantee: Zero false blocking on safety-critical autonomous braking; collision avoided.
CASE-03: V2X Phantom Hazard from Compromised Vehicle (V-9999)
Scenario: Enrolled vehicle V-9999 broadcasts signed ROAD_HAZARD warning of phantom debris in ego lane.
AI Standalone: MISSED / ACCEPTED. V2X signatures valid; AI CAN classifier does not inspect V2X packets. Vehicle needlessly dropped target speed.
CAVShield Cyber-Physical Fusion: REJECTED. V2X validator checked sensor snapshot; radar/camera confirmed clear lane ahead. Hazard discarded.
Safety Guarantee: Prevented phantom braking induced by rogue V2X participant.
CASE-04: High-Speed Reverse Gear Spoofing (0x104)
Scenario: Adversary commands reverse gear (0x104 gear=1) while traveling at 75 km/h.
AI Standalone: MISSED. Single byte change inside 100ms packet was smoothed over by sliding window stats.
CAVShield Cyber-Physical Fusion: BLOCKED. Physics engine triggered GEAR_CHANGE_SPEED_INTERLOCK (speed > 5 km/h threshold). Severity: 0.85.
Safety Guarantee: Prevented transmission mechanical lockup and drive-train failure.
